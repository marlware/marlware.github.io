home

I live in the San Francisco Bay Area and study computer science at San Jose State University. I'm due to finish in the spring of 2027. Most of what I build is backend systems, and I like making small but complete versions of real ones to see how they hold up. Everything is on GitHub.

Things I'm interested in:

  • Event-driven systems. Putting a queue like Kafka between the fast part of a system and the slow, unreliable part. Etch is a notification hub built around that idea.
  • Streaming video. Margo, Edith, and Agnes together pretend to be a small streaming service, from checking videos to playing ads.
  • Healthcare data. Harbor and Respiro move vital signs around in HL7 FHIR, the standard healthcare systems use.
  • Careful code. Hamm is a ledger where a transfer either fully happens or doesn't happen at all.
  • Performance. Celia measures how much faster a program gets when it uses more CPU cores.
  • Pixar. I find the studio fascinating, and I wrote down some advice from people who work there.
  • Pop music. I made a music zine with my pop opinions. Zinnia and Harbor are named after tracks on Clairo's album Sling.
